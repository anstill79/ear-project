Ear Project

Free browser-based tools for audiologists

Testing

  • Audiogram

    The best point and click audiogram on the web.

  • SAL

    Interactive worksheet to help avoid BC masking dilemmas.

  • Spot Check

    Use your smartphone to quickly check sound levels in your booth and save readings.

Devices & communication

  • ListeningCheck

    Listen to audio while adjusting hearing devices.

  • LOUD Text

    Handy communication tool for live typing in large font sizes.

Reports & referrals

  • HNS Referral Guidance

    Interactive decision tree for referring patients to HNS.

  • Vestibular Test Summary

    A very simple interactive form for summarizing peripheral tests of vestibular function.

Utilities

  • Screenshot Arranger

    Create a quick print/copy layout using screenshots.

  • Image Overlay

    Paste images to overlay and compare, drag, and adjust opacity.

Everything runs in your browser. No patient data is sent anywhere.
